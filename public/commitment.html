Our commitment to you

Watchora is built with and for blind and low-vision people. This page states, in plain language, what you can count on from us — and what you should never count on Watchora for.

1. Watchora helps. It never replaces your cane, dog, or training.

Watchora is a secondary assistive tool. It does not replace a white cane, a guide dog, orientation and mobility training, official pedestrian signals, or emergency services. It never tells you a crossing is safe, and it never authorizes you to cross a road.

2. We will not disappear on you.

Assistive tools have been sunset by big companies before, leaving users stranded. Watchora is an independent project and we commit to:

  • Export everything. Your saved places, contacts, and reading history can be exported at any time — your data is yours.
  • No silent shutdowns. If Watchora ever stops operating, we will give at least 6 months' notice inside the app and publish a full data export path.
  • Open core. The perception and safety logic is reviewed in public; the safety rules that matter are deterministic and testable, not hidden prompt magic.

3. Your privacy is the default, not a setting.

  • Hazard detection runs on your phone. The YOLO model processes camera frames on-device. Those frames never leave your phone.
  • Scene descriptions use the network, and say so. When you ask Watchora to describe a scene or read low-confidence text, that frame is sent to our server for AI analysis. The response always tells you when AI was involved.
  • Images are never stored. Frames sent for analysis are processed in memory and discarded. We log that a request happened, not what you looked at.
  • Location is consent-gated twice. A caregiver can only see your live location when you enabled sharing for that contact AND that specific journey.

4. When we are not sure, we say so.

Watchora speaks with calibrated confidence. When the AI is unsure, it says "I'm not fully sure, but…" and lowers its confidence. When the on-device detector is uncertain, it stays silent rather than guessing. False silence is safer than false certainty.

5. Emergencies are deterministic, and we tell you what actually happened.

The SOS flow is not AI. It is a deterministic, audited sequence. When you trigger SOS, we tell you which trusted contacts were notified and over which channel — we will never tell you someone was notified if the message did not go out.

6. You can always reach a human.

Questions, accessibility barriers, or a safety concern about Watchora itself: contact the team through the app's feedback entry, or file an issue on the public repository. Accessibility complaints are treated as priority bugs.
